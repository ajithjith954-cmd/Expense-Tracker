# Home

Mode: operate

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home screen is a mini-statement. One spent total leads, and the lines under it explain that total. It refuses a row of equal metric cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper ground, ink text, one bank-blue accent. A Material navigation bar. Rows separated by rules. Amounts set in tabular figures. The highest category is the only saturated mark.

STORY: The person reads the month's spending, income, and net, sees where the money went, and opens a recent payment to correct or delete it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The month is the title. The spent amount is the only display-size figure. Received and net are two text lines. A short exclusion sentence follows. Category rows and the first payments begin below, still on the same paper, with no card grid.

FORM: The sixth grounded direction, an ATM mini-statement, translated into a light Material column because the brief fixes a light, plain Android screen. Thermal gray, fake paper, and a monospace costume were dropped. Seed key 8f115b05.

RAISES: Specimen — scale contrast does the hierarchy, so only the spent total is large. Transit diagram — one chosen mark carries the color, so only the highest category uses the accent. Ticket wallet — a voided line stays on the page, so failed and excluded payments remain visible and are named.

SIGNATURE: A category bar draws once to its share of the month. Reduce Motion shows the final width immediately. Opening a payment slides to a detail screen where that same amount stays the largest type.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
